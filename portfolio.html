End-to-End Methodology

4-Stage Engineering Delivery Pipeline

From initial design inputs to shop-floor manufacturing drawings, every project undergoes rigorous CAD, DFM, and simulation verification.

STAGE 01
Scope & Inputs

Establish dimensional envelopes, functional loads, material selection, production volume, and machine capabilities.

STAGE 02
3D CAD & DFM

Parametric solid modeling, clearance checks, parting line definition, draft validation, and kinematic interference analysis.

STAGE 03
Simulation Audit

FEA stress and deformation evaluation, safety factor checks, CFD aerodynamic fields, and load-path optimization.

STAGE 04
Drawings & Release

2D manufacturing drawings with ASME Y14.5 GD&T callouts, BOM tables, surface finishes, and shop-floor setup sheets.
